    How to read this

    Current MP means current at the Parliament snapshot. It does not describe representation when the earlier GP extract was taken.

    Site location is an address allocation. It does not establish an ICB or trust catchment, service coverage or commissioning responsibility.

    Registered patients come from a GP practice list. They are not a census resident count or a measure of care received.

    Country coverage: constituency and MP records cover the UK. GP registrations cover England practices; GP addresses and some site reports include Wales. Trusts and commissioners are mainly England reports. Missing evidence in Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland does not mean no NHS services.

    Sources and method

    Derived from the NHS Geography Spine v0.3 JSON contract. UK Parliament current members, NHS England ODS organisation reports, NHS England GP registrations, ONS LSOA21 best-fit lookup and the NHS Postcode Directory each keep a separate date and role.

    Some codes remain unmapped or lack a current RE6 operator. Absence is shown with a reason; no location or patient population is inferred from a name.
